Prototype limits

This is a demonstrator, not a commercial geological modeller. It uses inverse-distance interpolation and simplified fault displacement. It does not perform seismic inversion, true 3D fault topology, stratigraphic gridding, uncertainty-calibrated inference, or engineering validation.
